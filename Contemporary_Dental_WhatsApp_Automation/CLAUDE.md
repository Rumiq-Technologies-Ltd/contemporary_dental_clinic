## Reminders specific to this folder

- Real patient/customer phone numbers are PII and outbound automated
  messaging is subject to consent/opt-out law (TCPA in the US, plus
  WhatsApp Business Platform's own messaging policies) — see the root
  `CLAUDE.md` note. Confirm the applicable regime and WhatsApp API terms
  with Mohammed before any real send.
- Use synthetic contact lists and placeholder message content in
  drafts/tests.
- No automated sends go live without explicit approval in the conversation.
